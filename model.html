What do these numbers mean?

The platform reports different metrics depending on whether the problem is classification or regression.

Cross-validationCandidate models are tested across multiple folds. Large datasets use a representative training subset to reduce compute while preserving an untouched holdout set.
Holdout validationAfter model comparison, the winning model is retrained on the full training split and evaluated on data that was never used for training or model selection.
Smart optimizationMemory-efficient numeric types, duplicate removal, categorical safeguards, and adaptive sampling reduce compute without changing the original upload.
